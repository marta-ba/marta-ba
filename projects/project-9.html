The question

Travel search data is rich, timely, and underused for storytelling. Official tourism statistics lag reality by weeks. The core question this project answers:

Which destinations are seeing search interest surge before it shows up in official accommodation statistics — and which are seeing confirmed growth across both datasets simultaneously?

Key findings — February 2026

  • 🇮🇹 Italy — the strongest confirmed tourism growth in the dataset (+17.0% YoY).
  • 🇦🇹 Austria — the quiet overperformer (+6.5% YoY, +700% search lag).
  • 🇫🇷 France — most interesting forward-looking story: search peaked at maximum index in Sept 2025, yet tourism growth remains moderate.
  • 🇵🇱 Poland — top of the momentum ranking thanks to a low search base producing dramatic relative growth.
Travel Momentum Score
Score = 0.6 × tourism_yoy_pct + 0.4 × search_yoy_pct_lag2m

A weighted composite blending confirmed tourism growth (60%) with forward-looking search momentum from 2 months prior (40%). An exploratory storytelling metric for editorial use — not a forecasting model. The 2-month lag was selected after testing correlations at 0, 1, 2 and 3 months.

Methodology

Data sources

  • Eurostat — Nights spent at tourist accommodation establishments, monthly, Jan 2024 – Feb 2026.
  • Google Trends — "flights to [country]" search interest, monthly, Jan 2024 – Apr 2026.

Cleaning & transformation

Eurostat numbers cleaned from EU thousand-separator format (dots) and flags (e, u, :) removed. Google Trends already at monthly resolution. Countries matched by standardised name across both sources.

YoY calculation: (value_month − value_same_month_prior_year) / value_same_month_prior_year × 100

Outputs

Four publication-ready CSVs land in data/datawrapper_exports/, each mapped to a Datawrapper chart type:

  • 01_momentum_score_ranked.csv — horizontal bar chart of ranked destinations.
  • 02_scatter_search_lag2_vs_tourism.csv — scatter plot of search vs tourism.
  • 03_search_index_over_time.csv — multi-series line chart of search trends.
  • 04_tourism_yoy_growth.csv — multi-series line chart of tourism YoY.

A one-page PR-ready insight report is generated to outputs/PR_insight_report.md — exactly what a travel media team needs to publish a data story.

Limitations & honesty

Google Trends values are relative (0–100 scale), not absolute. Low-base countries produce extreme percentage growth figures and need to be interpreted with care.

Correlation between search and tourism growth is weak (r = 0.04–0.07), so no causal claims are made. The Momentum Score is a storytelling lens, not a predictive model.
