A comprehensive analysis of operational and financial data from a service company in the utilities / technical services industry. The objective: move beyond reporting into decision intelligence — surface inefficiencies, financial risk and profitability drivers, and propose a scalable data-quality framework. All company-specific information has been fully anonymised.

The problem

The company had years of operational and financial data spread across Excel files, but no integrated view. Leadership could see what was happening — revenue, costs, projects — but not why some locations were dramatically more profitable than others, or how dependent the business really was on a small group of clients.

The brief: build an analytical system that goes beyond reporting and supports actual decision-making, and propose a data-quality layer that makes the system trustworthy over time.

Methodology

1. Data engineering (ETL)

  • Integration of multiple Excel-based datasets into a single source
  • Data cleaning and transformation in Python (pandas, numpy, openpyxl)
  • Pipelines structured for consistency and future scalability

2. Data analysis & modelling

  • Exploratory data analysis (EDA) across projects, clients and locations
  • KPI design and calculation in SQL (revenue, EBT %, cash-flow, billing efficiency)
  • Feature engineering for profitability and operational metrics

3. Visualisation

  • Interactive Power BI dashboards focused on business decisions, not vanity metrics
  • Geospatial and time-based analysis of operations

4. Data quality layer (strategic component)

  • Proposal for validation systems and anomaly detection
  • Monitoring framework for data consistency over time
Python 3.10 pandas numpy SQLAlchemy PostgreSQL SQL (KPI modelling) Power BI matplotlib · seaborn

Operational overview

  • 58 clients · 137 projects · 2.36 avg projects per client · 48 locations

Financial performance

  • Total revenue ~€12M · Net profit ~€7.4M · Total debt ~€7.18M · Annual growth ~€2.43M

Billing efficiency

  • 794 invoices · avg processing 40 days · avg payment delay −101 days → cash-flow inefficiency and delayed collections

Operational load

  • 75% open · 21.5% reserved · 3.1% cancelled → active workload but potential bottlenecks

Geographic distribution

  • Activity concentrated in a major metropolitan area, with limited international operations → opportunity for controlled expansion

Strategic recommendations

Immediate actions

  • Reduce backlog by prioritising open-case resolution
  • Diversify client portfolio to mitigate concentration risk
  • Replicate high-efficiency practices across underperforming locations
  • Optimise cost-heavy regions through pricing and resource allocation

Medium-term strategy

  • Improve billing-cycle efficiency to reduce payment delays
  • Align operational performance with profitability targets
  • Strengthen KPI monitoring for faster decision-making

Data-quality roadmap — the high-impact layer

Once the data-quality system is in place, the same dashboards unlock predictive profitability models, automated KPI-deviation alerts, real-time validated reporting, customer lifecycle segmentation, and automated anomaly detection — turning a static report into a living decision system.

Impact

This project demonstrates end-to-end thinking: data → insight → strategy. It integrates engineering, analytics and business logic with a strong focus on decision-making, not just reporting — while being honest about data limitations and scalability challenges.
