The problem

Brazilian federal agencies forecast their revenue collection annually. But year after year, certain agencies massively underperformed — collecting less than 10% of forecast — with no apparent consequences.

I asked: which agencies systematically fail? Is it improving over time? And what does this tell us about budget governance?

Methodology

  • Scraped and cleaned 7 years of public budget execution data
  • Calculated execution rate (actual / forecast) per agency, per year
  • Identified persistent underperformers (low execution across multiple years)
  • Tested for trend: was the system improving, deteriorating, or static?

Recommendations

Forecast accountability mechanisms

Recommended: agencies that miss forecasts by >50% for 3 consecutive years should be required to submit revised, defensible projections — and face budget rebalancing if they continue to miss.
