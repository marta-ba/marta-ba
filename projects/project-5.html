The problem

The wholesale division was celebrating record-breaking sales volume — but profit margins kept shrinking. Was high volume actually destroying value?

The CFO needed a clear answer to decide whether to push for more growth or pivot to margin protection.

Methodology

  • Cleaned 8,847 transaction records (2012–2014) in Excel + Power Query
  • Computed gross margin, contribution margin, and discount depth per transaction
  • Built KPI dashboard with conditional formatting and dynamic filters
  • Ran correlation analysis between order quantity and unit profit
Excel Power Query KPI Design Profitability Analysis

The paradox

Image — quantity vs profit scatter
Negative correlation (−0.43): bigger orders meant deeper discounts and lower per-unit profit.
Image — margin trend by year
Volume up 22%, margin down 18% over the same period.

Business impact

Reframed the growth strategy

Findings led to a tiered discount policy: above-threshold orders required margin approval. Within 6 months, average gross margin recovered ~4 points without a meaningful drop in volume.
