The problem

The marketing team was running broad, untargeted campaigns. They didn't have a clear picture of who their best customers actually were — only aggregate revenue numbers.

The goal: find distinct customer groups, profile them, and recommend which segments to target.

Methodology

  • Cleaned and joined demographic + transactional data
  • Exploratory data analysis to identify high-spend cohorts
  • Cross-tabulation by household composition, income, education
  • Tested whether differences were statistically significant

Findings

Image — spending by household composition
Households without children spent 50% more on premium products.
Image — campaign response by segment
Only 1 of 5 campaigns showed measurable response across segments.

Business impact

Three actionable segments identified

The team reallocated budget from blanket campaigns to targeted ones for the high-value "no children, high income" segment — projected to improve campaign ROI by ~30%.
