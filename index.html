About

I'm a data specialist based in Copenhagen, originally from southern Spain. My background is unusual: I started in sociology before moving into data — which means I ask different questions than most analysts. I care about the why behind people's behaviour in models, not just what the numbers say.

"Good data work is half technical, half human — you have to understand the business as deeply as the model."

Right now, I'm focused on process automation so I can spend more time improving data quality at the source. I'm especially interested in fintech, consumer insights and sustainability — fields where rigorous data work has real-world consequences.
