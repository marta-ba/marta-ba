About

I'm a data analyst based in Copenhagen, originally from Spain. My background sits at the intersection of social science and data engineering — a combination that makes me good at asking the right questions, not just running the right models.

I've built BI systems from scratch for companies across retail, construction, and market research — replacing spreadsheet chaos with structured, automated reporting. I'm drawn to contexts where data is still messy and decisions still need defending.

Right now I'm particularly interested in fintech and digital finance — how neobanks use behavioural data, how crypto markets can be modelled, and how financial products can be made more transparent through better analytics.

I'm open to remote-first roles in data analytics, business intelligence, and market research — ideally where the work is ambiguous enough to need real thinking.
