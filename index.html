About

I'm a data analyst based in Copenhagen, originally from southern Spain. My background is unusual: I started in sociology before moving into data — which means I ask different questions than most analysts. I care about why people behave the way they do, not just what the numbers say.

I've worked across retail, construction, market research, and product analytics — always in roles where the data was messy and the problem hadn't been properly defined yet. That's the work I find most interesting.

Right now I'm focused on fintech and consumer insight — how digital financial products can be made more transparent, how behavioural data can drive better product decisions, and how market research methods can be modernised with ML.

I'm open to remote-first roles in data analytics, BI, and consumer research. I work in English and am actively learning Danish.
